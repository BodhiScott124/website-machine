# Start here

Welcome. This folder is everything you need to build something real.

## What you're going to build

A machine that builds websites.

You point it at a small business — a plumber, a cafe, a pool shop, anyone with a bad old website — and it reads their existing site, works out what the business actually does, and builds them a much better one. In about a minute.

Then you show it to them and ask if they want it.

That's a real business. People pay properly for websites, and most small businesses have terrible ones. You're 16 with a tool that builds them in a minute.

## What you're actually learning

Not coding. You won't be writing code — Claude writes the code.

You're learning the two things that make the difference between people who build things with AI and people who have a folder full of half-finished projects:

**1. How to ask for what you want.** Being clear, saying what "working" looks like, and knowing how to describe a problem so it can be fixed.

**2. How to not lose the plot.** Keeping track of what you're building across days and weeks so that session twenty still knows what session three decided. This is the one almost everybody gets wrong.

Those two skills work on everything you build for the rest of your life. The website machine is just what you're building while you learn them.

## The plan

You'll start by building a website for **your photography business** — a real one, live on the internet, with your name on it. That should take an afternoon.

Then you'll build the machine that does that automatically.

Then, at the very end, you'll point the machine at your own photography site and watch it build a better version of the thing you started with. That's the fun bit.

There's no deadline. There's no schedule. Go as fast or as slow as you want, and stop whenever you want — everything you build is saved and waiting when you come back.

## What to do right now

**1. Get Claude Code on your PC.**

Open `guide/setup-and-accounts.md` and follow the first section. It's a download and a sign-in. You'll need your dad for this bit because there's a subscription — that guide explains exactly what to tell him.

**2. Open this folder in Claude Code.**

In Claude Code, click the **Code** tab, then **Select folder**, and choose this folder — the one this file is in.

**3. Say hello.**

Type this and press enter:

```
Hi! I'm new to all of this. Read CLAUDE.md and PROGRESS.md,
then tell me what we're building and what the first step is.
```

That's it. From there it'll walk you through everything.

## The one habit that matters

**At the end of every session, before you finish for the day, type this:**

```
Update PROGRESS.md with what we did and what's next, then save my work.
```

Ten seconds. It's the difference between coming back tomorrow to a project that knows where it was, and coming back to a mess.

Claude is told to do this automatically, but check it happened. It's your project.

## When you get stuck

You will get stuck. Everyone does, constantly — it's most of what building is.

First, tell Claude exactly what you're seeing and ask it to explain. That fixes most things.

If you're still stuck after about half an hour, **message Lucas.** Send him a photo of your screen and say what you were trying to do. That's what he's there for, and he'd rather hear from you early than find out three weeks later that you stopped.

Being stuck isn't failing. Quietly giving up is the only way to lose here.

Go build something.
